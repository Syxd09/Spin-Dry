# Fabric Sanctuary

Build a world-class premium website for a professional fabric care and laundry brand.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT use predefined templates or common laundry website layouts. Think from first principles and create a unique, memorable experience that immediately communicates professionalism, quality, trust, and convenience.

You have complete creative freedom over the UI, UX, animations, interactions, layout, typography, and visual identity. Do not ask for design preferences. Make the best possible decisions yourself.

The objective is to create a website that visitors enjoy exploring while still making it effortless to understand the company's services, expertise, and booking process.

--------------------------------------------------

BUSINESS PROFILE

--------------------------------------------------

This is a premium professional laundry and fabric care company.

It is NOT a neighborhood laundry shop.

The company specializes in caring for household and commercial fabrics using professional cleaning methods and equipment.

The business offers Pickup & Delivery within a 10 km radius from the store.

Customers outside the pickup radius can still contact the company for consultation or special requests.

The business provides professional cleaning services including:

• Curtain Cleaning

• Carpet Cleaning

• Blanket Cleaning

• Sofa Cover Cleaning

• Bedsheet Cleaning

• Comforter Cleaning

• Duvet Cleaning

• Pillow Cleaning

• Cushion Cover Cleaning

• Quilt Cleaning

• Table Linen Cleaning

• Home Linen Cleaning

• Commercial Linen Cleaning

• Hotel Linen Cleaning

• Office Fabric Care

• General Laundry Services

The company DOES NOT provide Wet Cleaning.

Do not mention or advertise Wet Cleaning anywhere.

--------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------

BOOKING SYSTEM

--------------------------------------------------

Create a complete booking workflow.

The booking system should allow customers to:

• Select one or multiple services

• Choose Pickup & Delivery

• Select preferred date

• Select available time slot

• Enter pickup address

• Automatically validate whether the address falls within the 10 km pickup radius

• Collect customer information

• Review booking

• Confirm booking

Support future integration with payment gateways.

Support future booking management.
